Key Data Analytical Insights

  • Direct Email Response Rate: 50% of contacted churned subscribers replied to exit surveys, confirming price hikes as the primary cancellation driver.

  • Geographical Clustering: 100% of September 2024 churn instances originated from Karnataka state due to localized price friction.
  • Support Escalation: Subscribers with 1+ escalated support complaints had a 100% churn rate vs 0% for non-escalated tickets.
