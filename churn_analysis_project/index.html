Key Data Analytical Insights

  • Contract Disparity: Monthly contract subscribers churn at 55.6%, compared to only 8.3% for Annual contract holders.
  • Geographical Clustering: 100% of September 2024 churn instances originated from Karnataka state due to localized price friction.
  • Plan Tier Vulnerability: Basic tier subscribers account for all churn events, with zero churn in Standard or Premium annual tiers.
  • Support Escalation: Subscribers with 1+ escalated support complaints had a 100% churn rate vs 0% for non-escalated tickets.

Actionable Growth Recommendations

  • Contract Migration Campaign: Offer a 15% discount incentive to convert Monthly Basic users into Annual Standard plans.
  • Proactive Resolution SLA: Implement a strict 24-hour SLA for support ticket escalations in high-density regions like Karnataka.
  • Win-Back Outreach: Deploy targeted email/SMS offers highlighting exclusive content to recovering former Basic subscribers.
  • Risk Score Priority: Automatically route customers with churn scores > 40 directly to senior retention specialists.
